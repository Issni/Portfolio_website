01

Overview

Apex Legends is a fast-paced battle royale with a distinct visual identity — yet its menu systems and HUD often feel cluttered and unintuitive. This project reimagines the core UI across three key areas: weapon customisation, progression, and in-game navigation.

The redesign prioritises clarity and hierarchy without stripping the game's signature gritty military aesthetic. Every screen was rebuilt in Figma using the existing brand language as a foundation.

02

Weapon Select

The weapon selection screen was redesigned to give each weapon more visual presence. A large side-panel preview replaces the original grid thumbnail, letting players inspect attachments and stats at a glance before entering a match.

Weapon categories are collapsed into a horizontal filter rail to reduce vertical scrolling, while key stats — damage, fire rate, range — are surfaced directly without entering a sub-menu.

03

Skins + Charms

Cosmetic browsing is one of the most-used screens in the game, yet the original layout buries rare skins behind multiple filter layers. The redesign introduces a featured showcase with a full-bleed weapon render and a sidebar browser for swapping skins and charms in real time.

Charm attachment points are visualised directly on the weapon preview, reducing the guesswork of where each charm sits in-game.

04

Progression

The Battle Pass and rank progression screens were consolidated into a single unified view. A persistent progress bar anchors the bottom of the screen, while the reward track unfolds horizontally with animated unlock states.

Season milestones and daily challenges are surfaced on the same page, removing the need to navigate between three separate sub-menus just to check daily progress.
