Overview

Apex Legends is a fast-paced battle royale with a distinct visual identity. This project reimagines the core UI across three key areas: weapon selection, weapon customisation, and progression.

As someone who plays a lot of fps games, I wanted to change up Apex’s armory. I wanted to change some UI up to create a smoother navigation flow while keeping the identity of Apex intact.

02

Weapon Select

I found the gun type selection of the armory difficult to navigate as the navigation grid took too many clicks and seperate pages to reach the desiered weapon. I wanted to simplify that into a side scrolling selection bar instead.

The original grid selection system has been changed to be a scrolling selection bar with each weapon for its specific type sorted under its name.

03

Skins + Charms

I found the charms tab being seperated from the skins tab which createdled to more navigation for the user. I combined these two pages into one with a side menu bar beside the skins tab.

The two side boxes allows for one-click simple navigation when it comes to selection.

04

Progression

As someone who was new to Apex, the leveling system was familiar as it was similar to other games but, I found it challenging to find the trials tab in this page. I moved the trials to the left side to be more visible to the user and more clear with what rewards will be received.
