About Project

As someone who loves to play games, Steam has been a website I use often to browse through games. Recently, I have been using the Steam app to look through games on the go, however, I have found that the user experience of the app could be improved.

02

Problem Statement

One of the main issues I had was the inconsistency of the colours, and icons used across the steam pages, and the app layout was hard to navigate.

The header and footer took up too much real estate on the page and lead to a crammed viewing experience.

03

Possible Solution

My goal is to create a cleaner, more organized interface with easier methods of nagivation
